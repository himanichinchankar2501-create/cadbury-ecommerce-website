History of Cadbury

Cadbury was founded in 1824 by John Cadbury in Birmingham, England. Initially, the company sold tea, coffee, and drinking chocolate. Over time, Cadbury expanded globally and became one of the most recognized confectionery brands in the world.

The launch of Dairy Milk in 1905 revolutionized the chocolate industry. Cadbury entered India in 1948 and gradually became one of the leading chocolate brands in the country through strong marketing strategies and emotional branding campaigns.
