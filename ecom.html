About the Website

This website was created as part of an E-Commerce project to understand the process of website creation and online business presentation. The website focuses on Cadbury, one of the world's leading chocolate brands. The purpose of this project is to demonstrate how companies use websites to promote products, interact with customers, and improve digital presence.

The website was developed using HTML and CSS technologies. HTML was used for creating the structure of the website, while CSS was used for designing and styling the interface. The project helped in understanding web development concepts, user interface design, and the importance of e-commerce platforms.

History of Cadbury

Cadbury was founded in 1824 by John Cadbury in Birmingham, England. Initially, the company sold tea, coffee, and drinking chocolate. Over time, it became one of the largest confectionery companies in the world. The launch of Dairy Milk in 1905 revolutionized the chocolate industry and helped the company establish a strong global identity.

Cadbury entered the Indian market in 1948 and gradually became one of the leading chocolate brands in the country. Through innovative marketing campaigns and emotional branding, the company successfully connected with consumers of all age groups.

Today, Cadbury operates under Mondelez International and continues to dominate the confectionery industry through innovation, sustainability, and digital transformation.
